# THINAI Agricultural Safety Policy & Invariants

## 1. Principle of Non-Harm in Agricultural Advisory

Automated machine learning advice in agriculture carries immediate financial, environmental, and ecological consequences for smallholder farmers. Misidentifying a fungal blight as a physiological deficiency can result in total crop failure, whilst unnecessary chemical application leads to soil degradation, pesticide resistance, water table contamination, and human health hazards.

THINAI operates under strict, software-enforced safety invariants designed to prevent harm under out-of-distribution real-world field conditions.

---

## 2. Core Safety Invariants

### Invariant 1: Strict Chemical Advice Gate
> **No automated chemical recommendation, pesticide active ingredient, or application dosage may be presented to a farmer unless the crop–disease pair is explicitly marked `FIELD_VALIDATED`, has achieved an empirical Expected Calibration Error (ECE) $\le 0.15$ on at least 50 real Indian field images, and has received human agronomic approval.**

- Currently, `ALLOW_CHEMICAL_ADVICE` is globally locked to `false` in `src/config/advicePolicy.ts` and `IS_G4_FIELD_VALIDATED` is locked to `false` in `src/services/diseaseDetectionService.ts`.
- All `LAB_ONLY` entries in `src/data/cropEvidenceRegistry.json` are capped strictly at **Tier 2 (Cultural sanitation and monitoring practices only)**.
- Any attempt to display chemical treatment for unvalidated pairs or models is rejected by `determineAdviceTier()` and `gateSafetyCriticalContent()`.

---

### Invariant 2: Weak Feedback Signal Guard
> **Farmer yes/no feedback constitutes a weak subjective signal of perceived usefulness. It MUST NEVER be treated as a ground-truth scientific disease label, and MUST NEVER be fed automatically into model retraining loops.**

- As proven in field epidemiology studies, farmers and non-expert field scouts misidentify complex leaf pathologies in up to 40% of cases (e.g. confusing early blight with septoria spot, or zinc deficiency with viral mosaic).
- Farmer feedback is recorded exclusively to calculate a `reliabilityScore` for the triage queue (`src/services/weakFeedbackService.ts`).
- Only diagnoses validated or corrected by a credentialed **Extension Officer** or **KVK Agronomist** can be promoted to verified training datasets (`canBeUsedForModelTraining: false` invariant guard).

---

### Invariant 3: Multilingual Safety & Dosage Gating
> **Pesticide chemical names, application rates (e.g. g/L, mL/L), waiting periods before harvest (PHI), personal protective equipment (PPE) requirements, and emergency toxicity protocols MUST NEVER be machine-translated without certified native agronomic human review.**

- `src/data/languageReviewStatus.json` registers all 23 Scheduled Indian languages.
- Only **English (`en`)** and **Tamil (`ta`)** have undergone certified agronomic human review (`safetyContentVerified: true`).
- For the remaining 21 Scheduled languages (`hi`, `te`, `kn`, `ml`, `bn`, `mr`, `gu`, etc.), safety-critical agronomic content is automatically replaced with human-verified English or Tamil text, and accompanied by the mandatory warning:
  > *"Safety-critical instructions are available in English/Tamil. Please contact an extension officer before using any pesticide."*
- Voice announcements use strictly static approved templates (`APPROVED_SAFETY_SPEECH_TEMPLATES`), preventing dynamic unverified dosage announcements.

---

### Invariant 4: Conservative Meteorological Rules
> **Weather data (temperature, relative humidity, rainfall probability, wind speed) reflects environmental conditions for spore incubation or drift risk, but MUST NEVER be claimed to confirm or diagnose the presence of an active pathogen.**

- All weather advisories generated by `src/services/cropWeatherAdvisoryService.ts` prepend the mandatory notice:
  > *"Weather conditions indicate environmental disease risk, NOT a confirmed diagnosis. Always inspect crop foliage directly before taking management action."*
- Spray window greenlights (`FAVOURABLE_SPRAY_WINDOW`) require wind speeds $< 15\text{ km/h}$, zero precipitation in the immediate forecast window, and acceptable temperature ranges ($15^\circ\text{C} - 32^\circ\text{C}$).
- When wind exceeds $15\text{ km/h}$ or rain probability exceeds $40\%$, the window is marked `UNSUITABLE_SPRAY_WINDOW` to prevent chemical drift or rain wash-off.

---

### Invariant 5: Universal Officer Safety Valve
> **Whenever model confidence is low, uncertainty is elevated, or the crop is unsupported, the system MUST gracefully degrade to extension officer triage rather than guessing.**

- Diagnosis cases with uncertainty $U > 0.65$ or probability $< 0.45$ are automatically routed to Tier 3.
- The UI presents direct contact options for the **Kisan Call Centre (Toll-Free `1800-180-1551`)**, local Assistant Agricultural Officer (AAO), or local Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK).
- Ambiguous cases offer an optional **3-Question Symptom Clarification Flow** (`src/services/symptomQuestionService.ts`) to gather structured phenotypic context before escalating to an officer.

---

## 3. Privacy & Consent Framework

THINAI adheres to data minimization and privacy-preserving design for smallholder farmers:

1. **Explicit Photo Sharing Consent**:
   - Before uploading any leaf image for officer review, the farmer is presented with 5 explicit choices via `src/services/photoConsentService.ts`:
     - Full photo
     - Cropped leaf only (strips faces and background)
     - Blurred background (obscures field surroundings)
     - Metadata only (no image pixels transmitted)
     - Do not share (stored only in local IndexedDB)
2. **Location / GPS Consent**:
   - GPS coordinates are never captured or uploaded without a separate, explicit opt-in checkbox.
   - Farmers may use the app anonymously or with manual district/taluk selection.
3. **Right to Withdraw Consent**:
   - Farmers can revoke sharing consent at any time via `withdrawPhotoConsent()`, which halts pending sync uploads and marks queued cases as withdrawn.
4. **Local-First Retention**:
   - Offline records reside in browser IndexedDB via Dexie.js and are synced to Firestore only when the user chooses and connectivity allows.
